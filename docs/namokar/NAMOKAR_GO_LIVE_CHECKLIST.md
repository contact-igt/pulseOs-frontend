# Go-live checklist (before staff start)

1. **Accounts** — each person has their own sign-in at `/login/namokar`: Owner, Receptionist, Coordinator A, Coordinator B, Dr. Poonam Jain. Passwords set by the administrator; demo passwords replaced.
2. **Clinic data** — real clinic name/address, Dr. Poonam Jain's working hours and the service list (Cataract, Oculoplasty, LASIK / refractive, Ptosis, General eye consultation) checked in Settings. Demo patients cleared (fictional data must not remain in a live workspace).
3. **Call outcomes** — the outcome list in Settings reads in the team's own words.
4. **WhatsApp** — add the Business account and approved templates in *Integrations*; send one real test confirmation to a staff phone; confirm the 1-hour reminder rule is enabled in Settings. Until then messages show as fixture.
5. **Calls (Runo/IVR)** — add Runo credentials, place one test call, check it appears with a recording for Owner/Admin only. Until then use **Log call**.
6. **Website form** — give the web team the intake token and the contract; submit one test enquiry.
7. **Train 30 minutes** — walk through the Daily SOP: Add Lead → Log call → Callback/Appointment → Confirm → Check-in → outcome.
8. **Run for a week with the owner reviewing** *Command Centre → Performance* twice, then raise anything confusing.
